How can I get my Konomiya card?

Visit one of the stores shown on the map and go to the Service Counter, which they call so, and ask a clerk who works within that that you want to purchase one, saying Puri-peido kaado wo kudasai. Then you shall receive the card and a manual along with it. The URL which I have shown on my website must be printed on them. (that is https://www.vcsys.com/s/konomiya/m/)
